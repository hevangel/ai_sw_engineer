## What does NOT exist (explicit gaps)

- No backend code, no target JSON, no runtime, no tests in this repo: there
  is **no working Rust/LLVM compiler for the MC6800**, and none is claimed.
- No Rust program has been compiled for the 6800; no compiler output has
  been executed; no ROM budget has been measured.
- The 6801/6301/68HC11 supersets are out of scope; 6809 is issue #62's
  subject; 68k is #64's.
